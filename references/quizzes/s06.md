# Session 6 quiz: Producing docs, decks, sheets and designs

Contents: Q1 to Q10, fill in the blank, matching.

Ask one question at a time. Show the stem and options only. Hold the key until the learner has answered. Do not repeat a question in the same sitting.

## Q1

The team needs a budget to total and sort. Which format?
- A. Doc
- B. Sheet
- C. Deck
- D. Whiteboard.

Key: B.

## Q2

First check of a generated deck?
- A. Fonts
- B. Every number and claim against the source
- C. Colours
- D. Slide count.

Key: B.

## Q3

Which are the seven parts of a brief?
- A. Title, author, date, font, colour, logo, footer
- B. Goal, plan, budget, risk, owner, date, status
- C. Prompt, model, effort, tools, memory, skills, hooks
- D. Audience, purpose, source material, length, format, what to exclude, acceptance check

Key: D. Why: A brief with all seven parts yields a usable first version.

## Q4

Docs can be exported as:
- A. Word (.docx), PDF and Markdown
- B. Only images
- C. Only .xlsx
- D. Only .exe

Key: A. Why: Docs export to .docx, PDF and Markdown.

## Q5

A sheet downloads as:
- A. .docx
- B. .xlsx
- C. .pptx
- D. .md

Key: B. Why: Sheets download as .xlsx.

## Q6

What lets Claude create and edit PowerPoint, Excel, Word and PDF files?
- A. Higher effort
- B. A browser connector
- C. The pre-built skills
- D. Longer prompts

Key: C. Why: The pre-built skills are how Claude makes a file in those formats.

## Q7

Who can change an editable doc, sheet or deck?
- A. Only Claude
- B. Only the person
- C. Neither once published
- D. Both the person and Claude, in the same file

Key: D. Why: Editable outputs can be changed by either.

## Q8

What is true of some of the new tools on Enterprise?
- A. Some are in beta
- B. None exist
- C. All are retired
- D. They need Haiku only

Key: A. Why: Some of the new tools are beta on Enterprise.

## Q9

Your account lists fewer output types than a colleague's. What should you do?
- A. Assume the colleague is wrong
- B. Check what your account lists, since it varies
- C. Reinstall the app
- D. Wait for the model to update

Key: B. Why: What your account lists varies, so check.

## Q10

Why check content before formatting?
- A. Formatting is not allowed first
- B. Content never changes
- C. A well-formatted deck with a wrong number is still wrong
- D. Claude formats better afterwards

Key: C. Why: Review order: content against the source, then formatting.

## Fill in the blank

- A brief has seven parts: audience, purpose, source material, length, format, what to exclude and the ____ check. Key: acceptance
- Review content against the ____ first, formatting second. Key: source

## Matching

Match each need to a format.

- A table you will total and sort = Sheet
- Text colleagues will comment on = Doc
- A talk = Deck
- Boxes and arrows = Whiteboard or design
