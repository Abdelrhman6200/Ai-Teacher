# Session 24 model answer: Expert capstone: ship, drill, teach

Open only after the learner has attempted the case in their own words.

## Case

A production system's answers degrade after a model change, and nobody on call wrote the system.

## Model answer

The runbook points to the eval and the traces. The on-call person reruns the suite on the new model, finds the regressed cases, applies the documented fix or rollback, writes the postmortem and adds the cases to the suite.
