# Companion study: Claude Academy

Claude Academy (academy.claude.com) replaced Anthropic Academy on 20 August 2026. It is free and holds courses, tutorials, role use cases and webinars. Catalog size per a third-party guide dated 2 November 2026: 27 courses, about 119 tutorials and about 150 role use cases. The Academy home page itself listed only three courses when fetched on 17 November 2026, so confirm every title below in the live catalog before you point a learner at it. Course names come from that guide; the session mapping is this course's own judgement.
